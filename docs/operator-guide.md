# Operator Guide

## First Deployment

1. Confirm the client's incorporation/legal-entity status is complete
   (route to `cloud-itonami-M6910` or local counsel first if not).
2. Register the client's intake: business type, target public function,
   prior filing history in Dominica if any.
3. Run the advisor in read-only mode against the Public Procurement and
   Disposal of Public Property Act, 2021 (Act No. 14 of 2021)
   requirements, administered by the Central Procurement Unit / Chief
   Procurement Compliance Officer / Public Procurement Board (Ministry
   of Finance, Economic Development, Climate Resilience and Social
   Security).
4. Compare the checklist against the client's current documentation
   (CIPO Certificate of Incorporation with automatic taxpayer
   registration confirmation, employee PAYE registration if the client
   has staff, and the Second-Schedule confirming/approving authority
   record its own declared contract value requires).
5. Enable gated filing-draft assistance once the Market-Entry Compliance
   Governor contract is trusted; actual submission always requires human
   sign-off.

## Minimum Production Controls

- client-owned data store for business/tax registration documents
- clear provenance (official portal/regulation citation) for every
  requirement surfaced
- approval workflow for any portal registration or filing submission
- independent re-verification that the confirming/approving authority
  recorded for a filing (per the Second Schedule: Head of Procuring
  Entity / Chief Procurement Compliance Officer / Public Procurement
  Board) actually meets what the bid's own declared contract value
  requires before any `:filing/submit` -- never trust a self-reported
  authority
- named referral relationship with Dominica-licensed counsel or a
  registered agent for anything beyond checklist/draft assistance
- monthly audit export
- disputes/appeals route to the Procurement Review Council as defined
  by the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Property Act, 2021
  (ss.61-73), not this actor -- it has no standing to file a complaint
  on the client's behalf

## Certification

Certified operators must prove data provenance, audit traceability, that
automated actions cannot bypass the Market-Entry Compliance Governor, and
a working referral relationship with Dominica-licensed counsel or a
registered agent for whatever licensed representation the law of
Dominica requires for actual public-procurement filings.
